Wind & tropical cyclone

Speed units are computed from exact definitions, never chained through rounded factors. Averaging-period conversions follow WMO/TD-No. 1555, which uses two different tables for two different jobs — the tool keeps them apart.

Moisture & comfort

Saturation vapour pressure uses the WMO/CIMO Magnus pair, which inverts in closed form, so dewpoint comes back algebraically with the same two constants and round-trips exactly. Water and ice share a prefactor, so the two branches meet exactly at 0 °C.

Two of these indices take radiation into account and three do not, which is most of what separates them. UTCI and ISO 7243 WBGT need a radiant input; heat index, humidex and apparent temperature assume it away.

These indices assess heat stress. They do not diagnose it. They describe an environment, not a person, so they take no account of age, acclimatisation, hydration, medication or existing illness. ISO 7243 is a screening method for occupational exposure. None of this replaces an official heat warning from your national meteorological service, or medical or occupational safety advice.

Pressure & altitude

Built on the ISO 2533 / ICAO standard atmosphere. Note that the ISA is frozen at R* = 8314.32, not the modern CODATA value — using CODATA here would disagree with every published ISA table.

Thermodynamics & stability

Bolton (1980) for equivalent potential temperature, Stull for the saturated adiabat, and Romps (2017) for an exact analytic LCL — solved here with the lower branch of the Lambert W function rather than approximated.
